About Me: From Vision to Value — Powered by Purpose, Built on Experience, Shaped by Innovation

With over 30 years of industry experience, I specialize in turning business visions into scalable, high-impact products and services. My career spans Retail Banking, Health Services, Corporate Auditing, Business Development, Manufacturing, and Software Development, delivering transformative outcomes for over 1,000 SMEs and enterprise clients across Europe and Africa.

What drives my work is a simple but powerful mantra: Understand. Innovate. Deliver (UID).

At the heart of my strategy is a commitment to AI-powered decision-making and automation. I help businesses leverage data and modern technologies to build smarter systems, anticipate market trends, and improve user experience at every stage of the product lifecycle.

My professional development has been shaped by globally recognized programs like the McKinsey Forward Program. I've also participated in product labs with Pendo, Panda, HP, AWS, and Microsoft Azure.

I’ve worn many hats—Product Manager, Product Owner, Scrum Master, Business Analyst, Systems Auditor, Technical Writer—always connecting business goals to agile execution through clear strategy and systems thinking.

As a founder, co-founder, and CEO, I also coach entrepreneurs and social impact leaders on how to scale.

My focus today is on empowering SMEs, startups, and mission-driven organizations through digital transformation. Whether it's optimizing operations or launching platforms, I deliver with integrity, clarity, and purpose.
